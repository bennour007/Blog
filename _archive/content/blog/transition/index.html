This page has been a draft since 2022, almost 5 years ago, time flies by and changes us. Molds us into ever evolving personalities and characters. Time takes people away from us, and very rarely does it provide comfort. Time, is the most terrifying thing a human can face.

But honestly this is not about time or about mortalty, nor about loss or suffering. These words about nothing in particular, a side-quest away from my ever increasing list of deliverables. I wanted to write something meaningful, then I realised I own this space, I can do what ever I want. Meaning disolves and it itself becomes meaningless against the arrow of time. Time, is the only legetimate idea in existence. It shapes our reality and our understanding of our own existence, yet we don’t know what it is. Some physisits think that maybe, it’s a quntum element, mostly related to gravity since gravity is the curvature of space AND TIME. Who the hell knows. We know one thing, that our perception of time tells us it moves in one direction, to the future. This direction is what gives the past meaning, and what identifies causality. However, this is only our perception as humans to time, and it may distort what our understanding to what it actually is or is not. The same thing can be said for what a mosquito precieves, it precieves reality on a specific spectrum of so that it can identify warmth, and therefore blood. A heat detector if you will. We are blind to that kind of perception. I hope one day I can understand what time is. I hope one day we discover that some entities somewhere in the universe or on this floating rock we call earth, know something about the flow of time. Maybe causality as a principle is not even a real one, and that everything everywhere and in every time point is connected some how. Maybe the pyramids were built in the future, to fix something in the past. I know it sounds crazy, but I’m actually trying to write a book about it. The story goes like this, a universal event happens in our reality, essentially the universe is going to rip and die. Smart people from the future use alien technology to go back to the past before the pyramids were built, these pyramids are essential to fix the universe in a specific point in the past before the actual problem starts, so they build them back then using their technology to fix the problem now. That’s why the pyramids exists. And that’s how time doesn’t.
